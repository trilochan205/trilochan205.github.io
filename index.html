A little about me

People at the heart.
Stories along the way.

“The best journeys are those that bring us closer to people, ideas and ourselves.”

I am Trilochan Subedi. Social work is an important part of how I see the world: with empathy, responsibility and a belief that small acts can help create meaningful change.

Beyond community work, I enjoy literature and the power of a well-told story. Reading and writing invite me to pause, reflect and understand life from different points of view. Travelling adds another dimension — new places, different cultures and memorable encounters that continue to teach me.

This website is a small window into those interests and the experiences that shape my journey.
